Application performance · Production support

Investigating recurring HIS application delays

Issue: Users experienced application lag and repeated “Wait for response” messages.

Investigation: Reviewed application, server and cloud logs and identified unnecessary temporary files being generated at approximately 9 GB per day.

Action: Shared the findings with the development team, explained the observed impact and coordinated the investigation while keeping the relevant teams updated.

Outcome: The developers addressed the application-side issue.

Skills: Log analysis, production troubleshooting, incident investigation, RCA and cross-team coordination.

AWS · IAM troubleshooting

Investigating restricted IAM permissions

Issue: An IAM user could not perform expected AWS tasks, including launching EC2 instances and accessing S3 buckets.

Investigation: Reviewed the user's IAM permissions and identified a separate inline policy that was restricting the required actions.

Action: Analysed the policy configuration and the permissions affecting the user's access, then identified the policy as the source of the restriction for follow-up correction.

Skills: AWS IAM, policy review, permission troubleshooting, EC2 and S3 access investigation.

Zabbix monitoring and alerting

Used Zabbix to monitor infrastructure health and identify server, network and service issues proactively, supporting faster investigation and operational visibility.
